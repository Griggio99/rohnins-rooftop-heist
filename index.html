CO-OP HEIST

Pick CO-OP in the game menu, create a room and send the 4-letter code. Your friend opens this same site and enters it. You run the same rooftops, see each other as live ghosts, share boss health and share 3 team lives. A downed agent respawns while the team still has lives, so cover each other. Bosses get tougher with two shooters, and the trailing player gets a catch-up boost.

👥

SHARED WORLD

Identical seeded rooftops, a named partner ghost, a live distance gap and a team score.

💥

SHARED BOSSES

Every point of damage either of you deals counts. Open the core together for double damage.

❤️

TEAM LIVES

Three lives for the pair. Spend them wisely: when they run out, you are out.

Be smart with your ammo

THE ARSENAL

Every gun except the pistol runs on limited ammo. Each boss resists some gun types and is weak to others, so read the weapon wheel and swap at the right moment.

🏙️

SIX DISTRICTS

Casino rooftops to the Big Chime tower. Every district has its own sky, hazards, music and more lanes.

👾

MINI-BOSSES + BOSSES

A mini-boss with a unique trick, then a full boss, in every district. They get tougher as you upgrade.

⚡

NEW HAZARDS

Trip-lasers, awnings, shootable mines, flame vents and spinning saws. Jump, slide, shoot or dodge.

💾

CHECKPOINTS

Reach a new district and it saves. Continue later, or pay chips to resume after a wipe-out.
